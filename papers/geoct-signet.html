GeoCT-SigNet: A Geometry-Aware Hybrid CNN–Transformer Network for Offline Signature Verification

Haider Ali, Momina Moetesum, Adnan Ul-Hasan, Faisal Shafait, M. Imran Malik
School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (SEECS), NUST, Islamabad, Pakistan · Deep Learning Laboratory, National Center of Artificial Intelligence (NCAI), Islamabad
Under review, IEEE Access, 2026 (preprint)

[Preprint PDF] [Code]

Abstract

Offline handwritten signature verification (OHSV) requires simultaneous modeling of fine stroke-level geometry and high-level semantic structure, yet neither convolutional nor transformer-based architectures achieve this independently. Recent hybrid CNN–Transformer models improve local-global integration but remain insensitive to high-frequency contour details critical for distinguishing genuine signatures from skilled forgeries. We propose GeoCT-SigNet, which embeds the contourlet transform (a multi-scale, multi-directional image decomposition) into a differentiable Siamese pipeline. A Frequency-Aware Feature Extraction (FAFE) block refines low- and high-frequency subbands independently, fusing them with deep semantic features via bidirectional cross-attention and a depth-wise feed-forward module. GeoCT-SigNet achieves state-of-the-art EER on BHSig-B (5.80%), BHSig-H (6.84%), and competitive performance on UTSig (18.99%). At the real-forgery operating threshold, it attains 0% FAR against CGAN-generated forgeries and ≤3.6% against VAE-generated forgeries, substantially outperforming CNN and transformer baselines. At only 4.13M parameters, it is 11× more compact than the closest hybrid baseline, establishing contourlet-based geometric priors as a promising direction for parameter-efficient, robust OHSV.
