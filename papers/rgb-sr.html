RGB Super-Resolution for Multispectral Remote Sensing Images

Haider Ali, Fabian Stahl, Maxim Dolgich, Ulrich Schwanecke, Adrian Ulges, Faisal Shafait
NUST, SEECS, Islamabad, Pakistan · RheinMain University of Applied Sciences, Wiesbaden, Germany
IEEE International Geoscience and Remote Sensing Symposium (IGARSS), 2025, Brisbane, Australia (Poster), pp. 3885–3889

[PDF] [DOI] [Code]

Abstract

The use of satellite remote sensing images is becoming increasingly popular due to the decreasing costs and high frequency of acquisition. However, it is often constrained by the low resolution (LR) of the data. To address this limitation, we propose an enhanced SWIN2SR transformer-based model for the super-resolution (SR) reconstruction of RGB images given Sentinel-2 (S2) satellite data, leveraging both its spatial and spectral resolution. Our model combines the SWIN2SR Residual SWIN Transformer Block (RSTB) with Non-Local Sparse Attention (NLSA) blocks and High Preserving Block (HPB) for high-quality SR reconstruction. The NLSA block enhances the model's ability to capture long-range dependencies, while the HPB focuses on preserving high-frequency information for superior reconstruction quality. We also introduce a novel combination of loss functions that increases texture preservation, structural integrity, and overall perceptual quality. Experimental results with multispectral S2 data show that our model achieves the best performance on perceptual metrics (LPIPS) and is perceived as superior by survey participants.
