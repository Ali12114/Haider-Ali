Non-Local Sparse Attention Based Multi-Image Super-Resolution

Haider Ali, Maxim Dolgich, Ulrich Schwanecke, Adrian Ulges, Faisal Shafait
NUST, SEECS, Islamabad, Pakistan · RheinMain University of Applied Sciences, Wiesbaden, Germany
IEEE International Geoscience and Remote Sensing Symposium (IGARSS), 2026, Washington, D.C., USA (Poster), pp. 2383–2388

[PDF] [DOI] [Code]

Abstract

Modern satellites often revisit the same region with high frequency, providing an opportunity for the use of Multi-Image Super Resolution (MISR) in remote sensing. However, temporal variations and weak inter-image correlations make effective utilization of multiple low-resolution observations challenging. While recent transformer-based methods improve multi-image fusion, attention mechanisms are typically applied only during fusion or reconstruction, leading to information loss during feature encoding. We propose an enhanced transformer-based MISR architecture that integrates non-local sparse attention (NLSA) into the encoding stage to capture long-range spatial dependencies across images. We show that explicitly capturing spatial dependencies through NLSA during the encoding stage makes random shuffling of the image sequence during training effective, leading to improved utilization of multi-image information and overall model performance. We also introduce a novel combination of loss functions that increases texture preservation and structural integrity. Experiments on the PROBA-V Kelvin dataset demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms state-of-the-art approaches.
