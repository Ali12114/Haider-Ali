I work on trustworthy, uncertainty-aware deep learning for satellite imagery. My research covers super-resolution of multispectral imagery, diffusion models with conformal uncertainty, and SAR–optical learning that stays robust under real cloud cover. I have two first-author papers at IEEE IGARSS (2025, 2026), one of them state of the art on the PROBA-V benchmark.

I am a Research Assistant at the Deep Learning Lab (DLL), NCAI, NUST, where I develop uncertainty-aware diffusion models for super-resolution, a cloud-robust SAR–optical segmentation network, and a unified super-resolution and wildfire burnt-scar mapping pipeline on Sentinel-2 imagery. I also work as a Machine Learning Engineer at Dcube, where I deploy computer vision and language systems and lead a team of five. Previously, I was a DAAD-funded Research Intern at the Computer Vision and Mixed Reality Lab, RheinMain University of Applied Sciences (Germany).

I received my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Pakistan (thesis: Offline Handwritten Signature Generation Using Generative Models, supervised by Dr. Faisal Shafait), and my B.S. in Software Engineering (Gold Medalist) from BUITEMS, Pakistan. In September 2026 I was selected for the IEEE GRSS IADF School on Computer Vision for Earth Observation in Italy.

I am applying for PhD positions starting Fall 2027. Please get in touch if my work fits your group.

  • Ongoing Research

    • Cloud-Robust SAR–Optical Semantic Segmentation from Clear-Sky Labels | 2026 – Ongoing
      A two-stream SAR–optical network trained on clear-sky images only, with a SAR-only head, evaluated on real cloud cover (CloudSEN12) and three further benchmarks. Manuscript in preparation.
    • Uncertainty-Aware Diffusion Models for Super-Resolution | 2026 – Ongoing
      Conformal prediction, wavelet-domain losses and multi-modality alignment for diffusion-based super-resolution that reports where its output can be trusted. Manuscript in preparation.
    • Multi-Spectral Super-Resolution and Wildfire Segmentation | 2026 – Ongoing
      A unified super-resolution and burnt-scar segmentation pipeline on Sentinel-2 imagery of U.S. wildfires. Manuscript in preparation.
